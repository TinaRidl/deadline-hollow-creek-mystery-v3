DEADLINE — The Hollow Creek Murder

You are the detective. Jamie Mercer, a gentle nineteen-year-old from Hollow Creek, has been found dead by the lake. The police first called it an accident. You are here to find out who really killed him.

Your objective: interview witnesses and suspects, investigate locations, collect evidence, read the spirit notes Jamie leaves behind, and finally enter the murderer’s name.

Who you are and what you can do

  1. Read the police report and Jamie’s story.
  2. Question the people of Hollow Creek. They will share what they know, but they will not solve the case for you.
  3. Search the lake, boathouse, school, garage, diner, and Jamie’s home for clues.
  4. Watch for Jamie’s ghost. He does not chat with you directly. Instead, he appears from time to time and leaves hints in a speech bubble. Every hint is saved in the Spirit File.
  5. When you think you know the truth, enter the killer’s name in the Solve tab.

How the Temperature Bar works

Low temperature = clear, direct, factual cues. Easier to finish in about 15–25 minutes.

High temperature = more creative, abstract, emotionally layered cues. Harder to finish, often closer to 60–90 minutes.

Important: the slider changes the style and difficulty of hints and interviews, not the identity of the killer.

Jamie’s story

Jamie Mercer was bright, kind, and quietly brave. He worked odd jobs, cared deeply for his mother, and believed the town could be better than the secrets it protected.

On the day before he died, Jamie became frightened. He had found something he could not ignore. He planned to tell the truth on Monday. He never got that chance.

The rest is for you to uncover.

Your mission

Find the truth behind Jamie Mercer’s death

Question the town. Search the places that mattered. Listen when the dead speak. Every fact you need exists somewhere in Hollow Creek — but nobody will hand you the answer.

You are the detective. The police report gives you a starting point. The people of Hollow Creek give you fragments. Jamie’s spirit gives you guidance. The final accusation is yours.

Jamie Mercer’s life

Jamie was nineteen. He helped his mother around the house, did occasional work in town, and carried a quiet sense of responsibility that made people trust him. He was not reckless. He was not looking for danger.

In the last days of his life, Jamie uncovered information that made him deeply uneasy. He wanted to do the right thing. He wanted to bring it into the open. That choice cost him his life.

How to solve the case

  1. Read the official discovery details in the Police Report tab.
  2. Use the Interviews tab to speak with people in town.
  3. Use the Locations tab to search for physical evidence.
  4. Review your Evidence File and Spirit File in the Case File tab.
  5. Enter the murderer’s name in the Solve tab when you are ready.
